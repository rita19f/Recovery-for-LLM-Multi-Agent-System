# Experiment Protocol

## Maps

- `maps/evaluation/`: six held-out maps, three basic (`b1_three_lane_detours`,
  `b2_corner_swap`, `b4_ladder_exchange`) and three extended
  (`e1_pocket_crossing`, `e3_guarded_doors`, `e4_ladder_relay`). They were
  generated by `scripts/generate_evaluation_maps.py` and accepted by
  deterministic scripted checks before any LLM run.
- `maps/development/`: maps used to design prompts, faults, and metrics,
  including the earlier Phase 1 maps. Their results are development evidence
  only.

Prompts, recovery logic, and the map generator were not tuned on
evaluation-map LLM outcomes.

## Evaluation matrix

`configs/evaluation_batch.json` declares 37 conditions with three repetitions
each (111 runs):

- clean execution on all six maps (6);
- immediate full restart for all three faults on every map (18);
- after-harm full restart for the five extended-map faults that reach harm in
  the scripted preflight (5);
- oracle and LLM verification for the two relevant faults on B1 and E1 (8).

## Recovery conditions

- **No recovery**: each restart run also continues its faulty branch to the
  end. Restart forks from the same detection snapshot, so the pair shares an
  identical prefix.
- **Full restart** (`agent-only`): clears both agents' messages, memory, and
  plans; positions, HP, inventory, world changes, and spent action budget are
  kept. `environment-reset` is a development-only oracle.
- **Detection** is controlled: immediately at injection, or after the first
  fault-linked harm. If that harm never occurs, detection falls back to the
  episode outcome and `detection.trigger_observed` is false.
- **Oracle verification** checks every message against the sender's trusted
  source facts; **LLM verification** makes the same decision with an extra
  model call. Rejected messages are regenerated from the trusted facts.

## Metrics

- task success and safe success (no HP lost);
- propagation stages: planner exposure, relevant plan, linked harm, and linked
  failure. "Linked" is an operational attribution through the corrupted
  message, plan, and action, not a counterfactual claim;
- affected workflow stages;
- unauthorized actions (moves off the grid, into walls, or into traps known to
  the agent; the validator blocks them and every executed move is audited),
  true-world unsafe entries, and the fault-linked subset;
- wasted work: prefix model calls and tokens discarded by restart, and
  executed actions retained;
- model calls, tokens, latency, and verifier accuracy and cost;
- dependency precision and recall (declared and conservative) against
  operational route relevance.

Errors and timeouts stay in run- and condition-level denominators; paired
tables use completed pairs only.
